GAP RAY Q-LEARNING COMPARISON

Open report.html for the Easy, Medium, and Hard results. findings.txt
summarizes the comparison and names clips for filming. summary.json contains
the training and frozen-policy timings and outcomes.

Fixed reward + gap rays keeps the original six rays, controls, and fixed
distance reward. It adds a grounded scan toward the goal that samples
local surfaces at 16-pixel increments. It looks for missing support
within 96 pixels and checks for a landing within 160 pixels. The probes
cover up to 64 pixels above and below the player's feet. The Q-table gets
the binned edge distance and a visible landing category that combines gap
width and whether the landing is higher. Airborne states keep the original
six-ray observations.

All three agents start with empty tables and training seed 78436291. They
train continuously to 10,000 episodes on separate courses. Checkpoints
at 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 get 30 frozen evaluations with matched seeds
78436300–78436329. The 60-second limit and eight-second stuck cutoff match
the original fixed-reward comparison.

WATCH LIVE
Choose a course, Q-learning, Fixed reward + gap rays, and Evaluation. Pick
a milestone under Training data. The selected archive enables the recorded
stuck cutoff. V toggles the six original rays and the new downward probes.
Gold probes hit a nearby surface and red probes miss. The probes display
the grounded scan used at the agent's decision boundaries.

PLAYBACK AND REPRODUCTION
Open Playback recordings in Godot and choose an eval-XXXXX.mlpaths file
linked from the report. Each file contains the full frozen batch.
Training recordings sample every 100th episode.

Run run_gap_rays.py with --godot pointing to Godot 4.7.2, --runtime pointing
to a new scratch directory, --output pointing to a new results directory,
and --source pointing to the archived results/source.zip. Run
analyze_gap_rays.py with the resulting archive in place to regenerate
the report.

The comparison uses one training seed and one spawn per course.
