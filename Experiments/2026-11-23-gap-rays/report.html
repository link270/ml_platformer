Matched conditions

Each gap-ray agent started from an empty Q-table with seed 78436291. Each frozen batch uses seeds 78436300–78436329. The level geometry, six actions, physics timing, fixed reward, learning settings, 60-second limit, and eight-second stuck cutoff match the fixed-reward comparison.

The additional traces sample the ground within a bounded area toward the goal. They encode runway to the next gap, gap width, and whether a visible landing is higher. The original air observations remain in use. These results cover one training seed and one spawn per course.
