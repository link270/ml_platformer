Protocol and interpretation

The ten training chunks use seeds 12345–12354 in the same order for both methods. Check-in seeds are 1012345–1012354; the final 30 evaluation seeds are 2012345–2012374. Training method order alternates each milestone. Each new worker resumes learned values and exploration settings, with an explicitly matched new RNG seed. All checkpoints are retained.

  • One paired training replicate; the per-chunk seeds are not independent full training replicates.
  • One check-in episode per milestone is a diagnostic, not a success-rate estimate.
  • Final evaluations use different RNG seeds on the same map and spawn; deterministic policies may repeat the same route.
  • The matched 9000-episode diagnostic was selected after observing final-policy failures; it is not a prespecified primary endpoint or independent best-checkpoint validation.
  • Best progress is sampled at decision boundaries, so brief between-decision peaks may be missed.
  • Reward returns have different scales and are not a cross-method performance score.

Verified: 20,000 training episodes, 20 scheduled check-ins, 60 final evaluations, 60 post-hoc diagnostic evaluations, 20 checkpoint milestones, and 340 recorded paths. All evaluation checkpoint hashes stayed unchanged. No previous personal saves were used or overwritten.
