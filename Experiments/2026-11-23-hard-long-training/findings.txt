WHAT 50,000 RUNS CHANGED ON HARD

Fixed reward found two new winning policies after the original 10,000-run
budget. The 15,000 checkpoint went 30/30 in 24.23 seconds, and the 30,000
checkpoint went 30/30 in 19.83 seconds. The latter is 1.40 seconds faster
than the original 5,000-run policy. The fastest, median, and mean frozen
winning times were identical for all three fixed-reward successes.

The improvement did not persist. Fixed reward went 0/30 at 20,000 and
25,000, then at every sampled checkpoint from 35,000 through 50,000.
At 50,000 all 30 attempts got stuck with 2.1% median best progress.

Lower late learning rate won 30/30 at 5,000 with a 32.28-second median.
Every sampled checkpoint from 10,000 through 50,000 went 0/30. It still
won 367 of its last 5,000 training runs. At the 50,000-run frozen
checkpoint all 30 attempts got stuck with 7.3% median best progress.

Across all 50,000 training episodes, Fixed reward won 980 times (1.96%)
and Lower late learning rate won 1,378 times (2.76%). The slower
learning-rate preset had more wins during exploration, while Fixed reward
had more successful frozen checkpoints and the fastest frozen route.

The first 10,000 training records matched the original experiment for both
variants after accounting for the recorded run budget. The 1,000-, 5,000-,
and 10,000-run Q-table hashes also matched. These results come from the
same training seed and fixed spawn. The frozen checks tell us what held at
5,000-episode intervals.
