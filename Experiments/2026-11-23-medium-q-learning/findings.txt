WHAT HELD UP ON MEDIUM

Course position was the only variant that cleared all 30 frozen evaluations
at every measured Medium checkpoint. It also won 71.4% of training attempts,
compared with 33.1% for fixed reward alone. Over the final 1,000 training
episodes the gap was 90.0% to 40.7%. On Easy the position-aware agent won
95.9% of training attempts and all of its last 9,401. Adding position still
helped on Medium, where the longest training win streak was 103.

Fixed reward remained a meaningful improvement over its direct comparison,
Longer exploration. It raised overall Medium training wins from 18.7% to
33.1%. Both final tables cleared 30/30, and the fixed-reward route took
20.52 seconds compared with 28.62 seconds, about 28% faster.

WHAT CHANGED

Fixed reward alone went 0/30 at 1,000 and 5,000 Medium episodes, then reached
30/30 at 10,000. On Easy it had won all frozen runs at 1,000 and 5,000 and
failed all at 10,000. The earlier Medium study with a different training
seed also found a winning fixed-reward checkpoint before a failed final
save. The strong checkpoint depends on the run.

The position-aware route did not get steadily faster on Medium. Its three
winning times were 19.80, 22.82, and 22.42 seconds. The 1,000-run save was the
fastest frozen policy in the Medium comparison. Fixed reward alone had the
fastest winning 10,000-run save at 20.52 seconds, ahead of position at 22.42.
Position was the stronger choice for consistent wins across checkpoints.

The original baseline also improved on Medium in the frozen checks. It got
stuck 30/30 at 1,000, cleared 30/30 in 23.92 seconds at 5,000 and cleared
30/30 again in 22.05 seconds at 10,000. Its final Easy checkpoint had failed
30/30. The empty baseline went 0/30 on both levels.

SIDE EXPERIMENTS

The small time penalty and higher discount failed every sampled frozen
Medium checkpoint. Richer horizontal rays won at 5,000 and got stuck at
10,000. The lower late learning rate recovered to 30/30 by 10,000 and ran
in 23.15 seconds, ahead of Longer exploration at 28.62 seconds. On Easy the
late taper had instead slowed the final frozen route.

TRANSFER WITHOUT RETRAINING

Both matched 5,000-run Easy policies won 30/30 on Easy. The exact Easy tables
were frozen and moved to Medium. Fixed reward won 22/30, with a 21.10-second
median win. Course position won 5/30, with a 23.75-second median win. This
shows why the extra position input can be valuable when training for a
specific course even when the resulting policy transfers less well. It
compares these two saved policies and one new layout.
