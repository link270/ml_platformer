LANDING MEMORY MADE HARD WINS MORE COMMON

The landing-memory variant won 1,534 of 10,000 Hard training episodes.
The grounded-only gap-ray preset won 2, and plain fixed reward won 146.
The new variant's first win came at episode 1,675 in 35.33 seconds.
Its fastest training win took 21.97 seconds and its winning median was
30.28 seconds. It won 262 of its last 1,000 training attempts.

The frozen policy went 0/30 at 1,000, 30/30 at 5,000, and 0/30 at 10,000.
Every win at 5,000 took 22.73 seconds. Plain fixed reward had a faster
5,000-run checkpoint at 21.23 seconds. The grounded-only gap-ray variant
went 0/30 at all three checkpoints. At 10,000, the landing-memory policy
reached 21.7% median best progress before falling, against 12.7% for
grounded gap rays. The gain was in finding winning behavior during
training. The final frozen checkpoint still lost the route.

A physics test verified that the remembered lip moves from far ahead
to near ahead as the player approaches, that vertical clearance
changes during the jump, and that touchdown clears the target even
before the next action decision. With landing memory disabled, the
original gap-ray preset's 1,000-run Hard checkpoint reproduced byte for
byte using the updated game code.
