Open report.html for the comparison and filming-notes.txt for the recommended clips.
summary.json includes the detailed training and frozen-policy metrics, including
the episode numbers of the fastest and representative median replay runs.

All eight trained variants started from empty Q-tables with seed 78436291 and
trained continuously through 10,000 episodes. Every 1,000-, 5,000-, and 10,000-run
checkpoint was evaluated for 30 frozen episodes with matched seeds 78436300–78436329.
The empty baseline ran only the 30 evaluations. Stuck detection was on for every
run and the overall timeout was 60 simulation seconds.

PLAY THE FOOTAGE
In the game, open Playback recordings, then Open recordings, and choose an
eval-XXXXX.mlpaths file. Use sequential playback to show a single block or
simultaneous playback to show multiple paths. The training.mlpaths files contain
every 100th training episode, including both wins and failures.

To watch a standard saved policy live, copy the selected checkpoint JSON under
a descriptive name into the personal checkpoint folder opened by the setup
screen's Folder button. Select Easy, the matching Q-learning experiment,
Evaluation, and Stuck detection on. The live tie-break seed may differ from the
recordings. The course-position experiment uses its own checkpoint schema and
its recorded episodes open in the standard viewer.

FILES
results/            Seven standard presets, each with three checkpoints,
                    per-episode training metrics, and three evaluation batches.
position-results/   Fixed reward plus the four-block course-position observation.
control-results/    Fresh baseline at zero trained episodes.
source.zip          Frozen standard and course-position project sources.
run.py              Reproducible runner. Give each invocation a fresh output
                    and runtime directory. Use --course-position for the extra
                    variant or --untrained for the empty-table control.
analyze.py          Validates checkpoints and recordings and regenerates metrics
                    from the three completed output folders.

All durations are simulation seconds. The first training win gives both the
episode number and the duration of that episode. The training rate shows
performance with exploration enabled; frozen evaluation shows the learned policy
without epsilon exploration. Repeated paths from the same fixed spawn are not
independent tests on new layouts.
