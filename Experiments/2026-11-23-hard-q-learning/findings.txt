HARD RESULTS AND FILMING PICKS

The strongest result from Easy and Medium flipped on Hard. Fixed reward +
course position won all 90 frozen evaluations on each earlier course. On
Hard it won 0/90 and only 2 of 10,000 training runs. Its best frozen
checkpoint by progress was at 5,000. All 30 attempts ended in falls, with
55.6% median best progress toward the orb.

Two Hard checkpoints won. At 5,000 runs, Fixed reward per block cleared
30/30 in 21.23 seconds. Lower late learning rate also cleared 30/30, with
32.28 seconds fastest and median and 32.54 seconds mean. At 1,000 and
10,000 every variant went 0/30. By 10,000, the fixed-reward agent was
falling around 13.3% of the way to the orb, and the slower learning-rate
agent was getting stuck around 7.3%. The empty baseline went 0/30.

Fixed reward improved overall training wins over the matched Longer
exploration preset on all three courses. The Hard improvement was 92 to
146 wins per 10,000 training runs. Lower late learning rate recorded the
most Hard training wins (162 of 10,000, including 105 in the last 1,000),
and still lost its winning frozen route at 10,000. The original baseline
won 32 training runs and failed all 90 frozen evaluations. Higher discount,
the time penalty, and richer horizontal rays also failed every sampled
frozen checkpoint.

Both 5,000-run Easy policies that won every frozen Easy evaluation died in
all 30 attempts on Hard. On Medium, those same frozen tables had won 22/30
with fixed reward and 5/30 with course position. On Hard, both made about
13% median best progress. The comparison covers these two source policies,
three fixed layouts, and one matched training seed.

The course is playable under the same actions and six-tick timing. The
project's scripted course check cleared Hard in 19.03–19.10 seconds across
three takeoff margins. That is a scripted route check, separate from the
learned-agent results.
